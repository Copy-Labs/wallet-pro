# Smart Wallet Pro - The Future of Web3 Wallets

Smart Wallet Pro is designed to make blockchain technology accessible to the 6 billion people who aren't yet using Web3. Built with cutting-edge Account Abstraction technology, it eliminates the traditional barriers of seed phrases, high gas fees, and complex transaction signing.

## 🔐 Security First Design

**Smart Accounts, Not EOAs**
Traditional wallets use Externally Owned Accounts (EOAs) that require seed phrases and manual gas management. Smart Wallet Pro uses ERC-4337 Smart Accounts that:
- Pay their own gas fees through gas sponsorship
- Support batch transactions and advanced features
- Enable social recovery and multi-signature security
- Work seamlessly with existing dApps

**No Seed Phrases**
Instead of vulnerable 12-word seed phrases, Smart Wallet Pro generates secure smart accounts that are recoverable through your email or social accounts (future feature).
