# Privacy Policy for Wallet Pro

**Last Updated: November 20, 2025**

## Introduction

Wallet Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our browser extension wallet application ("Service"). By using Wallet Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: When you create or import accounts, we store account names, addresses, and creation timestamps locally on your device.
- **Private Keys**: Encrypted private keys are stored locally using your password for protection.
- **Network Preferences**: Your selected networks, custom network configurations, and network usage preferences.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Transaction Data**: We collect and store transaction history, including amounts, recipients, timestamps, gas usage, and blockchain confirmations. This data is fetched from public blockchain networks and stored locally.
- **Network Analytics**: Usage statistics for different blockchain networks, including connection times, success rates, and performance metrics.
- **Application Usage**: Basic usage patterns to improve the application experience.

### Information from Third-Party Services

- **Blockchain Data**: Transaction and balance information from public blockchain explorers (BlockScout, Alchemy) and RPC providers (Alchemy, Ankr, Infura).
- **Price Data**: Cryptocurrency price information from CoinGecko for balance calculations and transaction cost estimates.
- **Analytics Data**: User behavior and application performance data through PostHog (if enabled).

## How We Use Your Information

### Core Wallet Functionality
- **Transaction Processing**: To send, receive, and track cryptocurrency transactions.
- **Account Management**: To create, import, and manage your wallet accounts.
- **Balance Tracking**: To display accurate account balances across multiple networks.
- **Security Features**: To implement password protection, auto-lock, and secure key storage.

### Service Improvement
- **Performance Optimization**: To improve application speed and reliability.
- **Network Intelligence**: To provide optimal network recommendations and gas price alerts.
- **User Experience**: To enhance the interface and add features based on usage patterns.

### Analytics and Research
- **Usage Analytics**: To understand how users interact with the application (if analytics are enabled).
- **Error Tracking**: To identify and fix application issues.
- **Feature Development**: To prioritize new features based on user needs.

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do Not Sell Your Data
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

### Blockchain Transparency
- **Public Transactions**: All cryptocurrency transactions are recorded on public blockchains and are inherently transparent. Transaction details (amounts, addresses, timestamps) are publicly visible on blockchain explorers.
- **Address Information**: Wallet addresses are pseudonymous but publicly visible when used for transactions.

### Third-Party Service Providers
We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our Service:

- **Alchemy**: For RPC services, smart account infrastructure, and gas sponsorship.
- **BlockScout**: For blockchain transaction data and verification.
- **CoinGecko**: For cryptocurrency price information.
- **Ankr and Infura**: For additional RPC provider services.

These providers are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
- **No Cloud Storage**: All sensitive data (private keys, transaction history) is stored locally on your device only.
- **Browser Storage**: We use browser extension storage APIs for data persistence.
- **No Server Storage**: We do not maintain servers that store your personal wallet data.

### Encryption and Security Measures
- **Password Protection**: Private keys are encrypted using your password with industry-standard encryption.
- **Auto-Lock**: Automatic wallet locking after periods of inactivity.
- **Secure Key Generation**: Private keys are generated using cryptographically secure random number generators.

### Data Retention
- **Local Control**: You have full control over data retention. Data remains on your device until you delete it.
- **Transaction History**: Stored locally and can be cleared through application settings.
- **Account Data**: Persists until you delete accounts or uninstall the extension.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Access and Control
- **View Data**: You can view all stored data through the application's interface.
- **Export Data**: Export your accounts, transaction history, and settings.
- **Delete Data**: Delete accounts, clear transaction history, or reset the wallet entirely.

### Privacy Controls
- **Analytics Opt-out**: Disable analytics tracking if implemented.
- **Network Permissions**: Control which networks and services the wallet can access.
- **Auto-lock Settings**: Configure automatic locking behavior.

### Account Deletion
- **Complete Removal**: Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.
- **Blockchain Data**: Public blockchain transactions cannot be deleted but become pseudonymous.

## Third-Party Services

### Analytics (PostHog)
- **Optional Tracking**: Analytics may be implemented to improve the service.
- **Data Collected**: Anonymous usage patterns, error reports, and performance metrics.
- **Opt-out**: You can disable analytics through application settings.

### Blockchain Services
- **Public Networks**: We interact with public blockchain networks which are decentralized and transparent.
- **RPC Providers**: We use multiple RPC providers for redundancy and performance.
- **Gas Sponsorship**: Transactions under $1 may be gas-sponsored through Alchemy's infrastructure.

## Children's Privacy

Wallet Pro is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## International Data Transfers

As a browser extension that operates locally on your device, your data primarily resides on your local machine. When we interact with international services (blockchain networks, RPC providers), data may be transferred internationally as part of normal blockchain operations.
